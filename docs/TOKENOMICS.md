# ChitChain (MST) Tokenomics & Distribution

## Token Utilities
- **Collateral Backing**: MST can be locked as stake/reputation multiplier for higher-tier circles.
- **Dividend Sharing**: Circle organizer fees (0.5% - 1.5%) accumulate in the Protocol Treasury and are distributed to MST lockers.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Governance staking allows voting on defaulted circle liquidations.

## Round Settlement & Fee Flow
```
Total Round Pot = (Contribution Amount * Members)
Winning Bid Discount = (Discount Percentage * Total Pot)
Organizer Fee = 1% of Discount
Dividend Pool = 40% of Organizer Fee
Net Winner Payout = Total Pot - Winning Bid Discount
Dividend Paid to Non-Winning Members = Remainder of Discount Distributed Equally
```
