# ChitChain Technical & Domain Glossary

### Terms
- **ROSCA**: Rotating Savings and Credit Association — a group of individuals who agree to meet for a defined period in order to save and borrow together.
- **Chit Circle**: A decentralized smart contract instance representing a single savings group with fixed cycles and membership.
- **Foreman / Organizer**: The address that initializes the circle and coordinates dispute parameters.
- **Pot**: The pooled collection of periodic member contributions available to be claimed each cycle.
- **Discount Bid**: The percentage of the pot a member agrees to forfeit in order to claim the payout early.
- **Dividend**: The discount amount redistributed proportionally to all non-winning circle participants as interest earnings.
- **MST**: Mutual Savings Token — the native governance and collateral multiplier ERC-20 token.
